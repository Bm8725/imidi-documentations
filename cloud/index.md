# mySpace Account Setup

**mySpace** is your personal cloud space on iMIDI. It gives you access to **MyCloud** storage, where your sound banks, presets and custom maps are kept and synced across your devices.

One iMIDI account also gives you access to TS4X and E-Market.

## Setting Up Your Account

1. [Create an account](https://www.imidi.co.uk/register) with **Get Started**, or [sign in](https://www.imidi.co.uk/login) if you already have one.
2. Open [mySpace](https://www.imidi.co.uk/dashboard/cloud-db) from the main navigation.
3. Sign in with the same account on every device you want to keep in sync.

## What You Can Store

- Sound banks
- Presets
- Custom maps, including KORG/Genos formats
- Performance maps
- Synth patches
- System states, custom patch layers and layout configurations

## Storage

MyCloud provides a secure **30 GB** container. The MyCloud Storage plan is listed at **$49.9 per year**. You can manage it on the [MyCloud page](https://www.imidi.co.uk/mycloud).
