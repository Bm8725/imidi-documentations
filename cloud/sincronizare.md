# Audio Profile Sync (KORG/Genos)

MyCloud keeps your audio profiles in sync across every device you are signed into. Changes are synced instantly.

## What Is Synced

| Content | Details |
|---|---|
| **Sound banks** | Including KORG/Genos formats |
| **Presets** | Your custom presets |
| **Custom maps** | Including KORG/Genos formats |
| **System states** | Saved states of your setup |
| **Patch layers and layouts** | Custom patch layers and layout configurations |

## How It Works

1. Sign in with the same iMIDI account on each device.
2. Your content is pushed automatically to your MyCloud storage.
3. The same content is then available on every device you are signed into, in real time.

## TS4X

TS4X features lossless cloud synchronization and uses a hybrid architecture: it runs locally, with metadata kept on the server.

## KORG/Genos Formats

Sound banks and custom maps in **KORG/Genos formats** are included in the automatic backup and sync. You can keep your existing KORG and Genos content in the same MyCloud space as your other presets.
