# Secure 30GB Backup

MyCloud Storage is a secure **30 GB** container optimized for active audio profiles. You can back up your work directly from the stage.

## Summary

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Capacity** | 30 GB |
| **Price** | $49.9 per year |
| **Backup** | Automatic |
| **Sync** | Instant, across every device you are signed into |

## What Is Backed Up

- Sound banks
- Presets
- Custom maps, including KORG/Genos formats
- Performance maps
- Synth patches
- System states

## Managing Your Storage

Manage or upgrade your storage on the [MyCloud page](https://www.imidi.co.uk/mycloud). You need to be signed in to your iMIDI account.
