# Listing Rules & 14-Day Trial

## Summary

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Trial period** | 14 days |
| **Cost during trial** | Free to list |
| **Earnings during trial** | You keep 100% |
| **After the trial** | Optional promotion for a small fee, to keep the listing featured |
| **Buyer contact** | Buyers message you directly |

## What You Can List

- Presets
- Sound banks
- MIDI maps
- Scripts
- Physical units and instruments

## 14-Day Trial

Listing on E-Market is free for the first 14 days. During this initial trial period you keep 100% of your earnings.

## After the Trial

After the trial period you can promote a listing for a small fee to keep it featured.

## How a Sale Works

1. A buyer finds your listing through classic search or AI Smith.
2. The buyer sends you a message.
3. You agree the details and close the deal directly with the buyer.
