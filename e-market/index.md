# E-Market Seller Guide

**E-Market** is the built-in marketplace of iMIDI. Artists buy and sell presets, sound banks, scripts and instruments directly with each other.

## What You Can Sell

- **Presets**
- **Sound banks**
- **MIDI maps** (device maps)
- **Scripts**
- **Physical instruments and units**

## Getting Started

1. [Create an iMIDI account](https://www.imidi.co.uk/register) or [sign in](https://www.imidi.co.uk/login). One account gives you access to TS4X, E-Market and the i-volution controller.
2. Open [E-Market](https://www.imidi.co.uk/e-market) and select **Start Selling**.
3. Publish your listing. Listing is free for the first 14 days.

## How Buyers Find You

Buyers can find your listing in two ways:

- **Classic search**
- **AI Smith** (see [AI Smith Integration](/e-market/ai-smith))

Buyers message you directly to close the deal.

## Earnings

During the initial trial period you keep **100%** of your earnings. After the trial you can promote a listing for a small fee to keep it featured. See [Listing Rules & 14-Day Trial](/e-market/listare) for details.

## Backing Up Your Work

Your sound banks, presets and custom maps (including KORG/Genos formats) can be backed up to **MyCloud**, which provides 30 GB of secure storage.
