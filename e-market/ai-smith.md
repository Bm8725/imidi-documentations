# AI Smith Integration

**AI Smith** is one of the two ways buyers can find your listing on E-Market. The other is classic search.

## How It Fits In

| Discovery method | Description |
|---|---|
| **Classic search** | Buyers search E-Market for presets, sound banks, scripts and instruments |
| **AI Smith** | Buyers find listings through AI Smith |

Whichever way a buyer finds you, they can message you directly to close the deal.
